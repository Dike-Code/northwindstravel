Heritage Journeys

Diaspora and ancestry-rooted travel to Kenya, East Africa, and beyond.

Heritage Journeys is travel that goes home — for diaspora families, blended families, and travelers tracing roots. We weave genealogy work, family gatherings, and meaningful country travel into a single itinerary.

Perfect forThis journey suits

  • Diaspora families returning home, often together
  • Adult children traveling with parents to ancestral places
  • Families planning a multi-generational reunion abroad
  • Travelers researching genealogy and heritage

What’s includedEvery Heritage Journeys engagement

  • Genealogy and ancestry research where requested
  • Coordination with extended family on the ground
  • Cultural advisors and translators
  • Multi-property staging for large family groups
  • Reunion event planning and documentation
