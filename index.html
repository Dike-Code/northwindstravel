FAQ

Common questions, honest answers.

Do I need to know exactly where I want to go?

Not at all. Many of our best journeys start with a feeling — a mood, a milestone, or a destination you have always imagined. Share what you have in mind and we will shape the rest.

How far in advance should I reach out?

For most trips, four to eight weeks ahead is ideal. For larger group travel or more complex journeys, three to six months gives us the best options. That said, we have pulled together exceptional last-minute trips too.

Do you work with international destinations?

Yes. We plan travel across Africa, Europe, the Caribbean, and beyond. Whether you are returning home, exploring for the first time, or coordinating family travel across continents, we are experienced in exactly that.
